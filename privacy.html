Privacy Policy

Universal Smart TV Remote

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Universal Smart TV Remote ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, processes, shares, and protects information when you use our Android and iOS mobile applications.

By using the App, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Universal Smart TV Remote allows users to discover, connect to, and control compatible smart televisions and streaming devices.

Depending on the features you use, the App and its service providers may collect or process:

  • App interactions and feature usage information;
  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers;
  • Approximate location inferred from network information;
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information;
  • Device model, operating system, and app version;
  • Subscription purchase history and verification information;
  • Selected photos and videos transferred during casting;
  • Text entered using the remote keyboard;
  • Voice input processed through Android or iOS speech-recognition services;
  • Location metadata that may be embedded in selected media;
  • Information about connected TVs and streaming devices;
  • App settings and saved-device preferences.

Some information is processed locally, while other information may be transmitted to service providers or compatible devices to deliver the App's features.

2. Local Network Access and TV Connections

The App uses your local Wi-Fi network to discover, connect to, and communicate with compatible smart TVs and streaming devices.

Information processed for these purposes may include:

  • TV names, models, and manufacturers;
  • Local IP addresses and network identifiers;
  • Device capabilities and connection status;
  • Pairing information;
  • Remote-control commands;
  • Information used to reconnect to saved TVs.

These operations primarily take place between your mobile device and compatible devices on your local network.

We do not use your TV's local network information to create advertising profiles.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Universal Smart TV Remote displays advertisements to users of the free version of the App.

We use Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers;
  • IP addresses and approximate location information;
  • Interactions with advertisements and the App;
  • Device and operating system information;
  • Diagnostic and performance information;
  • Information used for advertising measurement, fraud prevention, and security.

Depending on applicable law, device settings, and consent choices, this information may be used to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, measure advertising performance, and prevent advertising fraud.

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent.

Users may be able to manage applicable privacy choices through the consent options presented in the App and through their Android or iOS device settings. On Android, ads may be requested through AdMob; iOS ad requests remain unavailable unless valid iOS AdMob identifiers are configured.

Premium subscribers do not receive any applicable advertisements within the App while their Premium entitlement is active.

For more information, visit: Google Privacy Policy .

4. Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to improve its functionality and reliability.

Firebase Analytics may process:

  • App interactions and usage events;
  • App installation and session information;
  • Device and operating system information;
  • App-instance identifiers;
  • Approximate geographic information;
  • Information about feature usage and performance.

We use analytics information to evaluate App performance, understand feature usage, and identify areas for improvement.

The App does not intentionally send TV keyboard text, selected photos, or selected videos to Firebase Analytics.

5. Firebase Crashlytics and Diagnostics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify and investigate crashes, errors, and technical problems.

Crashlytics may collect:

  • Crash reports and error information;
  • Device model and operating system version;
  • App version and installation identifiers;
  • Technical diagnostic information;
  • Information associated with App stability and performance.

This information helps us diagnose problems, fix errors, and improve the App.

Firebase services are provided by Google.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security .

6. Premium Subscriptions and Purchases

Universal Smart TV Remote offers optional Premium subscriptions that unlock additional features and remove all applicable advertisements.

Android subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. If subscriptions are offered in the iOS app, Apple processes those transactions through the App Store.

We do not directly collect or store your full credit card number, bank account details, or payment-card security code.

To verify purchases and manage Premium access, the App may process:

  • Purchased subscription product identifiers;
  • Purchase tokens;
  • Subscription status and entitlement information;
  • Information required to verify purchases, renewals, cancellations, and expirations.

Purchase information may be transmitted securely to our subscription verification service.

Our verification system may retain information such as purchase-token fingerprints and subscription entitlement status to verify access and prevent unauthorized use.

Subscription purchases are optional. Users may continue using the free version without purchasing Premium.

Google Play manages subscription billing and payment processing according to Google's applicable policies.

7. Photo and Video Casting

The App allows users to select photos and videos from their mobile devices and cast them to compatible televisions or streaming devices.

Photo and video casting is initiated by the user. The App does not automatically upload a user's photo or video library to our servers.

When casting supported local media, the App may temporarily make the selected file available to the chosen receiver through a local HTTP connection.

The selected media is transferred to the compatible device to enable playback or display.

Selected photos and videos may contain metadata, including information about when or where the media was created.

If a selected file contains embedded GPS location metadata, that information may be transferred with the media to the receiving device.

The App does not currently guarantee removal of all embedded location metadata before casting.

We do not intentionally use the contents of cast photos or videos for advertising or analytics.

Receiving devices may process, buffer, or retain media according to their own functionality and privacy practices.

8. Voice Recognition

The App provides an optional voice-input feature that allows users to speak text instead of typing it manually.

When a user activates voice input, the App uses Android or iOS speech-recognition functionality.

Depending on the Android or iOS device and the installed speech-recognition provider, audio may be transmitted to external servers for processing.

The speech-recognition provider may process voice recordings and return recognized text to the App.

The App uses the recognized text to provide the requested keyboard-input functionality.

We do not intentionally upload voice recordings to our own subscription verification backend or Firebase Analytics.

Voice recognition is optional. Users can use the keyboard without activating voice input.

The applicable speech-recognition provider may have its own privacy and retention policies.

9. Remote Keyboard and User-Entered Content

The App allows users to enter text using a remote keyboard and send it to compatible televisions.

The App may also allow users to enter media URLs for supported casting features.

This information is transmitted to the selected device to perform the action requested by the user.

We do not intentionally collect remote keyboard text for advertising or analytics.

Users should avoid entering sensitive information through a TV connection they do not trust.

10. Device Permissions

Depending on the Android or iOS version and the features used, the App may request access to:

  • Local Wi-Fi and network connectivity;
  • Nearby devices and device discovery;
  • Photos and videos selected for casting;
  • Microphone or speech-recognition functionality when voice input is activated;
  • Other permissions necessary for supported device connections.

Certain Android permissions related to Wi-Fi discovery may be classified by Android as location-related permissions.

The App uses these capabilities to discover and connect to compatible devices, rather than to track the user's GPS location.

Permissions can generally be reviewed through Android or iOS device settings.

11. Saved Devices and Preferences

The App may store information about previously connected televisions and user preferences on the mobile device.

This information may include:

  • Saved TV connection information;
  • Previously paired devices;
  • App appearance and accessibility settings;
  • Remote-control preferences;
  • Other local configuration information.

This information helps the App remember your settings and reconnect to devices.

12. How We Use Information

We process information for purposes including:

  • Providing smart TV discovery and remote-control features;
  • Enabling optional photo and video casting;
  • Supporting remote keyboard and voice input;
  • Verifying Premium subscriptions;
  • Displaying advertisements to free users;
  • Measuring advertising and App performance;
  • Diagnosing crashes and technical problems;
  • Preventing fraud and unauthorized access;
  • Maintaining security and reliability;
  • Improving the App and its features;
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

13. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information directly to data brokers.

Information may be shared with or processed by third-party services where necessary to provide App functionality.

These services may include:

  • Google AdMob for advertising and related measurement;
  • Google Firebase for analytics and crash reporting;
  • Google Play for subscription billing and verification;
  • Cloud infrastructure providers supporting purchase verification;
  • Android speech-recognition providers for optional voice input;
  • Compatible TVs and streaming devices selected by the user.

Google AdMob may collect and share certain advertising-related information as described in Section 3.

User-selected media and keyboard content may be transmitted to compatible devices when users request those actions.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

14. Data Retention

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods depending on their purpose.

Saved TVs and App preferences may remain on your device until removed, cleared, or the App is uninstalled.

Subscription verification information may be retained as needed to manage entitlements, verify purchases, prevent fraud, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play, speech-recognition providers, and other service providers may retain information according to their applicable retention policies.

Media casting is initiated by the user. The App does not intentionally maintain a permanent server-side library of cast photos or videos.

Receiving televisions and streaming devices may have their own buffering, caching, and retention practices.

15. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to protect information processed by the App.

Communications with supported cloud services, including subscription verification, use encrypted connections where implemented.

However, some local network communications with compatible TVs and streaming devices use protocols that do not provide end-to-end encryption.

For example, selected casting media may be transferred to a TV using HTTP over the local network.

Users should connect to trusted Wi-Fi networks and compatible devices.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
