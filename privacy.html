Privacy Policy

Universal Smart TV Remote

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Universal Smart TV Remote ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Universal Smart TV Remote mobile application.

1. Information the App Uses

Universal Smart TV Remote is designed to allow users to discover, connect to, and control compatible televisions and streaming devices.

To provide these features, the App may process technical information including:

  • Devices discovered on your local network;
  • TV or streaming device name, model, manufacturer, IP address, and supported capabilities;
  • Connection status and connection information;
  • Information required to remember previously connected devices;
  • App configuration and preference information;
  • Technical information necessary for troubleshooting and maintaining app functionality.

Much of the information used to discover and control televisions is processed locally on your device and local network.

2. Local Network Access

The App uses local network access to search for and communicate with compatible smart TVs and streaming devices connected to the same network as your mobile device.

Local network information may be used for device discovery, establishing a connection, sending remote-control commands, reconnecting to previously connected devices, and determining device capabilities.

3. Device Permissions

Depending on your device and the features you use, Universal Smart TV Remote may request permissions required for functionality such as:

  • Accessing nearby devices and the local Wi-Fi network;
  • Discovering compatible televisions and streaming devices;
  • Communicating with devices over your local network;
  • Using compatible hardware features, where available.

Permissions are used only when required to provide the corresponding App functionality.

4. Saved TVs and App Preferences

The App may store information about televisions or devices you have previously connected to so that they can be identified and reconnected more easily.

Settings such as appearance, accessibility preferences, haptic preferences, and other App configuration may also be stored on your device.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Universal Smart TV Remote may offer paid subscriptions or premium features. Purchases made on Android are processed by Google Play. Purchases made on iOS, when available, are processed by Apple through the App Store.

We do not directly collect or store your full credit card or bank account details. Payment information is handled by the applicable app store and its payment systems.

We may process purchase status, product information, transaction information, and subscription entitlement information where necessary to determine whether premium functionality should be available to a user.

6. Analytics and Diagnostics

We may use limited analytics and diagnostic information to understand App performance, identify crashes or technical problems, improve functionality, and understand how features are used.

If third-party analytics or crash-reporting services are introduced or changed, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

7. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App may be used to:

  • Discover and connect to compatible TVs and streaming devices;
  • Provide remote-control functionality;
  • Remember connected devices and user preferences;
  • Provide and verify premium subscription access;
  • Diagnose technical problems;
  • Maintain security and reliability; and
  • Improve the App and its features.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the App, process purchases, provide infrastructure, diagnose technical issues, or comply with legal obligations.

For example, purchases may be processed by Google Play or Apple's App Store, depending on the platform used.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored settings and saved-device information may remain on your device until they are removed through the App, cleared through your device settings, or the App is uninstalled.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to their applicable policies and legal requirements.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

Universal Smart TV Remote is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

12. Third-Party Devices and Services

Universal Smart TV Remote communicates with televisions, streaming devices, platform services, and app stores that may be operated by third parties. Their products and services may be governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Universal Smart TV Remote changes or as legal and regulatory requirements evolve.

When this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
